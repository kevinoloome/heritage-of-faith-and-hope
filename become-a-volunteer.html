Become A Partner › Become A Volunteer

Join a close-knit family, not a rota.

When you volunteer at Heritage of Faith and Hope, you become part of the daily life of the centre — and that shows up in the children you spend time with.

Where Volunteers Help

Bring a skill, or just bring your time.

  • — Tutoring and homework support, Play Group through Senior School
  • — Health, nutrition, and hygiene education sessions
  • — Counselling and mentorship (for qualified volunteers)
  • — Sports, arts, music, and holiday programmes
  • — Maintenance, facilities, and hands-on project support

How It Works

Three steps to your first visit.

STEP 1

Reach out

Call, WhatsApp, or email the centre to tell us your availability and what you’d like to help with.

STEP 2

Orientation

A short briefing on our child safeguarding practices and daily routine before you start.

STEP 3

Begin volunteering

Join on an agreed schedule — a single visit, a term, or an ongoing commitment.

Ready to volunteer?

Reach us directly and we’ll help you find the right fit.
