Step by Step

How to give via M-Pesa.

STEP 1

Open M-Pesa on your phone

Go to Lipa na M-Pesa from your SIM Toolkit or the M-Pesa app.

STEP 2

Select Buy Goods and Services

Choose this option rather than Send Money.

STEP 3

Enter Till Number 494114

Double-check the digits before confirming.

STEP 4

Enter the amount, then your M-Pesa PIN

Confirm to send — you’ll get an M-Pesa confirmation SMS immediately.
