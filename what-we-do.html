What We Do

Four pillars, one child at a time.

Housing, schooling, health, and healing — delivered together, because children don’t experience their needs one at a time either.

01 · Residential Care

A home, not a holding place.

Orphans and vulnerable children (OVCs) live in a safe, family-style residential setting at the centre in Mlolongo — with consistent caregivers, structure, and the stability that recovery depends on.

02 · Education

Play Group to Senior School, on one campus.

We run Play Group, Pre-School, Primary School, Junior Secondary School, and Senior School up to Form 3 — giving children continuity in one place instead of moving between institutions as they grow. Where a child is ready for tertiary study, we support that path too.

03 · Health & Nutrition

Well enough to learn, well enough to play.

Every child receives regular medical check-ups, balanced daily meals, and ongoing health education, so that physical wellbeing never becomes the barrier to everything else we’re trying to build.

04 · Psycho-social Support

Healing what the street left behind.

Trained staff provide emotional and psychological support, helping children work through trauma and build the resilience they’ll carry into adult life.
