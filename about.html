About

Founded on compassion, run on discipline.

Heritage of Faith and Hope began with two people, thirty-eight children, and a conviction that every child deserves more than survival.

Our Story

From 38 children to a registered institution.

Rev. Joseph and Teresia Waweru founded Heritage of Faith and Hope Children’s Rehabilitation Centre on 30 August 2002, opening their doors to 38 children who had been living on the street. What started as one family’s act of compassion has grown into a legally registered Charitable Children’s Institution (CCI) under the Government of Kenya, based in Pridelands, Mlolongo.

Over the years, more than 300 children who passed through the centre have gone on to build careers, complete their education, and build stable lives of their own — the clearest evidence we have that the model works.

Rev. Joseph & Teresia Waweru · Founders — add real portrait

Mission

To transform the lives of orphans and vulnerable children through care, quality education, and spiritual grounding.

Vision

To be a comprehensive centre for the care, protection, empowerment, and transformation of children and youth.

Register · A Timeline

Twenty-plus years, in entries.

30 AUG 2002

The centre opens its doors

Rev. Joseph and Teresia Waweru take in 38 former street children in Mlolongo.

EARLY YEARS

Formal registration as a CCI

The centre is legally registered as a Charitable Children’s Institution by the Government of Kenya.

GROWTH

Education expands on-site

Play Group, Pre-School, Primary, and Junior Secondary School are established on the campus.

TODAY

Senior School and beyond

Education now runs through Form 3, with over 300 alumni and an active community outreach programme.

In the Community

Our work doesn’t stop at the gate.

Beyond residential care, the centre runs outreach programmes and awareness campaigns, and partners with local organisations on projects that improve child welfare across Mlolongo and the wider community.
