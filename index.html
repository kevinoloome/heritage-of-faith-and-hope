What We Do

Comprehensive care, from arrival to independence.

We meet four needs together, because a child cannot heal, learn, or grow on only one of them.

01

Residential Care

A safe, family-style home for orphans and vulnerable children (OVCs), away from the street and the risks that come with it.

02

Education

Play Group through Senior School (currently to Form 3), with a path toward tertiary education for every child who is ready.

03

Health & Nutrition

Regular medical check-ups, nutritious daily meals, and ongoing health education for every child in our care.

04

Psycho-social Support

Counselling and emotional support that helps children process trauma and build lasting resilience.
